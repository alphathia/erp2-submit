## P002

- **Section:** Abstract

- **Relevance:** benefit

- **Text:**

> Our results demonstrate that LLM-based test generation, when guided by static analysis, can be competitive with, and even outperform, state-of-the-art test-generation techniques in coverage achieved while also producing considerably more natural test cases that developers find easy to understand.
