> We introduce Robin, a novel multi-agent conversational AI-assistant within GitHub Copilot Chat, specifically designed for debugging.


## P002

- **Section:** Abstract

- **Relevance:** benefit

- **Text:**

> Through a within-subjects user study with 16 industry professionals, we find that equipping Robin to-(1) leverage the insert expansion interaction pattern, (2) facilitate turn-taking, and (3) utilize debugging strategies-leads to lowered conversation barriers, a 2.5 x improvement in bug localization and a substantial 3.5x improvement in bug resolution compared to AI-assisted debugging in Visual Studio prior to Robin.
