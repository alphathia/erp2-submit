## P002

- **Section:** Abstract

- **Relevance:** capability_claim

- **Text:**

> Our results show that UX-LLM cannot fully replace traditional usability evaluation methods but serves as a valuable supplement particularly for small teams with limited resources, to identify issues in less common user paths, due to its ability to inspect the source code.
