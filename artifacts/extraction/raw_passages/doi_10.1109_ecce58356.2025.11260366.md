> This paper introduces Circuit-AI, an AI-driven design assistant that leverages large language models (LLMs) to enhance efficiency in power electronics design. By integrating natural language processing with engineering workflows, Circuit-AI streamlines critical tasks such as Bill of Materials (BoM) optimization and circuit simulations through tools like LTspice and MATLAB.
