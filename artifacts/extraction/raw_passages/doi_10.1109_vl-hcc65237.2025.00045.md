## P001

- **Section:** Abstract

- **Relevance:** benefit

- **Text:**

> In a formative evaluation with 14 programmers involved in two debugging tasks, we found that those using Co-Advisor to receive context-related feedback alongside expert strategies were significantly more successful than those without context-related feedback. They demonstrated greater engagement and had an improved learning experience, gaining insight into the reasons behind their mistakes, correcting them, and understanding the rationale behind their actions.
