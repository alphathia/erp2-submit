## P001

- **Section:** ABSTRACT

- **Relevance:** benefit

- **Text:**

> Through a mix of qualitative and quantitative analyses, we found that developers are most motivated to use AI programming assistants because they help developers reduce key-strokes, finish programming tasks quickly, and recall syntax, but resonate less with using them to help brainstorm potential solutions.


## P002

- **Section:** 5 USABILITY OF AI PROGRAMMING ASSISTANTS

- **Relevance:** challenge

- **Text:**

> The most frequent usability challenges participants reported encountering were understanding what part of the input caused the outputted code, giving up on using the outputted code, and controlling the tool’s generations (#4).
