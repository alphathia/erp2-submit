## P005

- **Section:** 5. Discussion

- **Relevance:** challenge

> A key issue is the possibility that AI will automate many routine tasks, potentially reducing the demand for programmers in certain roles. The rapid development of AI technologies may require professionals to adapt to new concepts and tools, while also facing ethical questions related to the responsibility for AI-generated code and data security.
