## P001

- **Section:** 1 Introduction

- **Relevance:** capability_claim

- **Text:**

> Our tool introduces CodeHinter, an interactive debugging tool that goes beyond automatic error correction to actively guide students through diagnosing and fixing their own code. By integrating fault localization techniques with LLM-powered hints directly into the IDE through a simple one-click interface, CodeHinter promotes step-by-step problem solving, deepens students’ understanding of errors, and reduces reliance on fully automated solutions.


## P002

- **Section:** 3 Design of CodeHinter

- **Relevance:** interaction_mode

- **Text:**

> Instead of directly providing an explanation, the LLM generates three possible solutions, with only one being correct. These options suggest possible ways to correct the error, whether syntactical or semantic. Once the user selects an answer, the system immediately indicates whether the choice was correct and provides an explanation. By incorporating interactive quizzes, this feature encourages users to think critically while still receiving structured support.


## P003

- **Section:** 5.2 Post-Task Survey Results

- **Relevance:** usage_pattern

- **Text:**

> As expected, ‘End-to-End Test’ was used by all participants and ranked as the most frequently used feature. Among the four available helper tools, ‘Locate Lines With Errors’ was the most popular, with most participants using it multiple times, except for one user. The ‘Provide Hint and Quiz’ and ‘Insert Print Statements’ features were each used by half of the participants.
