## P001

- **Section:** 1. Introduction

- **Relevance:** usage_pattern

- **Text:**

> We have conducted a survey among more than 180 students which shows that at least 40% of respondents have used generative AI tools for solving homework on at least one occasion. At the same time, only 28% of students believe that this has benefited them in actual learning or in a better understanding of the assignment.


## P002

- **Section:** 1. Introduction

- **Relevance:** challenge

- **Text:**

> We have found that only 70% of generated tests are correct, and not even follow-up questions can improve this result. Our labs do not provide tests for students in our assignments. Thus, when a test fails, students may find it difficult to determine whether the issue lies with the solution or in the test supplied by ChatGPT.

## P004

- **Section:** 4. ChatGPT for code reviews

- **Relevance:** benefit

- **Text:**

> Using our tool, we have queried select pieces of code from 67 homework assignments and asked whether they have been written in functional style. The code pieces ranged in size from 1-2 to a record of 55 lines of code, for the same implementation task. Approx. 77% of the queries received complete and correct answers, containing valid argumentation.
